# Interface design

The interface is an operational research terminal: information density and explicit provenance take priority over promotional storytelling. The dark financial-terminal direction is required by the brief.

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** graphite-black `#101310`; raised work surfaces `#151a16` and `#1a201b`; fine dividers `#303832` and `#252d27`.
- **Accent:** muted chartreuse `#c2e879` for navigation state, signal paths, and primary action; restrained sage and amber distinguish secondary signal categories.
- **Typography:** system UI sans-serif for reading; system monospace for timestamps, identifiers, measurement labels, and status.
- **Composition:** persistent left navigation on wide screens; compact horizontal navigation on smaller screens; ruled sections, compact data tables, and an open chart workspace rather than nested cards.

## Page patterns

- **Overview:** coverage rail, synthetic signal chart, sector ledger, and fictional company watch table.
- **Companies:** searchable and sector-filterable entity register with explicit fictional-data context.
- **Infrastructure map:** abstract node-and-link schematic with keyboard-operable sample nodes and togglable compute, network, and energy layers.
- **Dispatch:** research-letter concept with a local-only preview form; no mailing-list integration.

## States and responsive behavior

Active navigation, hover, keyboard focus, map selection, muted map layers, search results, empty results, and form confirmation have visible states. At narrow widths the side rail becomes horizontal navigation, dashboard regions stack, and wide tables scroll horizontally.

## Data integrity

Sample badges and page-level disclaimers identify illustrative entities, chart values, signals, and network links. No live-data or investment-performance claim is part of this interface.
